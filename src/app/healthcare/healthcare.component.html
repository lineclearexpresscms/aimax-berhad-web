Healthcare

Business Overview

AIMAX Healthcare Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian healthcare manufacturing company specializing in the production of high-quality latex gloves for domestic and international markets. Strategically located in the northern region of Peninsular Malaysia, the company is supported by strong logistics connectivity, enabling efficient supply chain management and global distribution capabilities.

Equipped with 8 double-former production lines with a production capacity of up to 6.9 million pieces of gloves per day, Aimax Healthcare is committed to delivering safe, reliable, and high-performance healthcare protection solutions that meet global quality and regulatory requirements.

Through continuous investment in advanced technology, process improvement, and workforce development, Aimax Healthcare strives to become a trusted global glove manufacturer by providing consistent product excellence, sustainable manufacturing practices, and long-term value to customers, partners, employees, and stakeholders.

Business Units

NeoGenix Laboratoire

NeoGenix Lab is a medical diagnostic and research laboratory providing more than 76 tests ranging from infectious diseases, oncology, precision medicine, genetic tests and etc. NeoGenix Lab also offers a wide range of customised life science services in the field of research and development, such as DNA and RNA extraction, bioanalyser, tapestation and gene expression microarray services, Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) and bioinformatics analysis.

NeoGenix Lab is one of the earliest private laboratories which has obtained the Malaysia Ministry of Health's approval in providing the Covid 19 molecular testing and real time RT PCR to detect the presence of the SARS CoV 2 virus and to diagnose the active Covid 19 in assisting the country's effort to flatten the curve of infection.

HS Bio LiGNO

A joint-venture company with LIGNO Holding Sdn Bhd ("LiGNO") to market and distribute the LiGNO's products which aid in building resistance against lung-related and other health issues including, without limitation the Covid-19 virus.

LIGNO through its related companies is a company specialising in cultivation of medical herbs and manufacturing of supplements. Its cultivation and production facilities are certified and licensed including but not limited to ISO 22000, HACCP, GMP, Halal, and Organic Certified, whereas its products have undergone extensive research with research partners from around the world. LIGNO is also the world's first Tiger Milk Mushroom ("TMM") cultivation expert, as certified by The Malaysia Book of Records.

eMedAsia

Our partnership with Malaysian Medical Association in eMedAsia Sdn Bhd provides the Group an initial foray into the healthcare industry by leveraging on eMedAsia's medical digital platform to tap into the market for supply of Medical and Healthcare Products to eMedAsia and its member clinics, doctors and their patients. HS Bio is working closely with our strategic partner for the 3PL and 4PL fulfilment services.
