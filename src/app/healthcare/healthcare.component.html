Business Overview

AIMAX Healthcare Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian healthcare manufacturing company specializing in the production of high-quality latex gloves for domestic and international markets. Strategically located in the northern region of Peninsular Malaysia, the company is supported by strong logistics connectivity, enabling efficient supply chain management and global distribution capabilities.

Equipped with 8 double-former production lines with a production capacity of up to 6.9 million pieces of gloves per day, Aimax Healthcare is committed to delivering safe, reliable, and high-performance healthcare protection solutions that meet global quality and regulatory requirements.

Through continuous investment in advanced technology, process improvement, and workforce development, Aimax Healthcare strives to become a trusted global glove manufacturer by providing consistent product excellence, sustainable manufacturing practices, and long-term value to customers, partners, employees, and stakeholders.
