Corporate Overview

AIMAX Berhad aims to transform into a formidable listed company by expanding into glove manufacturing, medical/healthcare supply chain management, financial services (moneylending) and etc.

The Group continues to scale up its capabilities in line with the unprecedented challenges posed by the global pandemic, while maintaining its market visibility through persistent marketing efforts. AIMAX is committed to delivering quality services and maintaining good customer retention, supported by a team of talented and experienced industry professionals.

Our vision: becoming one of the notable glove makers in the nation, an end-to-end integrated healthcare supply chain specialist, and a provider of capable & reliable business financing solutions.
